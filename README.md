# Steam-Game-Sales-2024-2025
Analysis of revenue, positive reviews, and total reviews

# Overview
Transforming Kaggle dataset (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) into Power BI. Used Power Query to transform the raw data to visualise 2024 and 2025 revenue, 2024 and 2025 top 10 most positively reviewed games, and 2024 and 2025 top 10 most reviewed games.

# Software
Microsoft Power BI
